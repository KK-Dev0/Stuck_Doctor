Safety

A nudge, not a doctor.

Stuck Doctor is built to get you to care, never to replace it.

Stuck Doctor will

  • Help you understand why you (or a loved one) are avoiding it
  • Give one tiny, doable first step
  • Explain report terms and show which values fall outside the printed range
  • Turn your doctor's written instructions into reminders
  • Flag warning signs that mean "go sooner"

Stuck Doctor won't

  • Diagnose what's wrong with you
  • Suggest medicines, doses or home remedies of its own
  • Read X-ray, MRI or CT images (that needs a trained doctor)
  • Book appointments or know a doctor's availability
  • Replace a real doctor or pharmacist, ever

Is it an emergency?

Chest pain, trouble breathing, heavy bleeding, fainting or signs of stroke: don't wait. Get help now.
