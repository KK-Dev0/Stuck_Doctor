Why we wait

Putting off the doctor isn't laziness. It's six different problems.

Each one needs a different first step. Pick the one that sounds like you.

How it works

From "I'll do it later" to "done" in three taps.

Tell it why

Say what you're avoiding and pick the reason. Or upload a prescription, report or clinic card you've been ignoring.

Get your prescription

Gemma names the kind of stuck you're in and gives one 2-minute move, the best time to do it, and questions to ask.

Tap to act

Send the booking message on WhatsApp, find the right doctor on Maps, and set a calendar reminder, without typing a thing.

Safety

A nudge, not a doctor.

Stuck Doctor is built to get you to care, never to replace it.

Stuck Doctor will

  • Help you understand why you're avoiding it
  • Give you one tiny, doable first step
  • Write the booking message for you
  • Point you to the right kind of doctor nearby
  • Flag warning signs that mean "go sooner"

Stuck Doctor won't

  • Diagnose what's wrong with you
  • Suggest medicines, doses or home remedies
  • Interpret your test results
  • Book appointments or know a doctor's availability
  • Replace a real doctor, ever

Is it an emergency?

Chest pain, trouble breathing, heavy bleeding or fainting: don't wait. Get help now.
